About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web application designed to help you capture and organize your ideas quickly. Whether you're brainstorming, jotting down reminders, or keeping track of important information, QuickNotes provides a user-friendly platform to manage your notes efficiently.

Getting Started

To start using QuickNotes, simply navigate to the home page and begin adding your notes. You can view your notes in a list format and keep track of the total number of notes you've added.


How to Use QuickNotes

  1. To add a new note, type your note in the input field and click the "Add Note" button.
  2. Your notes will be displayed in a list below the input field.
  3. You can view the total number of notes you have added at any time.

Features

  • Simple and intuitive interface for quick note-taking.
  • Real-time display of your notes as you add them.
  • Count of total notes to keep track of your ideas.
